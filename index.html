A word from the owner

Built so every guest feels looked after.

My elder brother and I opened Kalash Dham in 2018 because we were tired of watching travellers on the Bagodar road hunt for a clean room and a proper meal at night.

I still remember our first guest, a truck driver from Ranchi who asked for tea at midnight. We woke the cook and made it fresh. That is how we still run the place: someone is always awake, and nobody is sent away hungry.

Come as a family, come on business, or come only for dinner. We will treat you the same.

— Owner, Hotel Kalash Dham
